How to play

No timer, no fail. Just put things where they live.

  • Drag anything off the floor and drop it where it belongs.
  • While you're holding something, the places that will take it light up.
  • Drop it somewhere wrong and it just wobbles back — nothing breaks.
  • Tap Where does this go? if a thing has you stumped.
  • Tidy a room without a single wrong drop for three stars.
