How to play

A quiet map-building game. There is no way to lose.

Creative town — the main game
  • Build whatever you like: any tile, anywhere on the map.
  • Pick a landscape from the palette, then tap the edges of the tile wheel to paint them — or tap its middle to set the ground underneath.
  • 🪣 fills the whole tile at once, ⟳ spins it, 🎲 rolls a surprise tile.
  • Tap the map to lay the tile. Tapping an existing tile replaces it.
  • 🖌 lets you drag to paint a whole run of tiles; turn it off to drag the map around.
  • 🧽 removes tiles, ↶ undoes, ⌖ re-centres, 🗑 starts over (tap twice).
  • Paint River or Rail on a couple of edges to run them through a tile — fill a whole tile with River to make a lake.
  • Harmony tells you how many touching edges agree. It is a compliment, never a rule.
  • Your town saves itself as you go.
Random mode
  • The stack randomises the layout for you — you place what you are dealt.
  • Tap a dashed space to lay the tile; ⟳ (or tap the tile) rotates it.
  • Every edge matching its neighbour scores. Rivers and rails are worth more.
  • A perfect fit — every touching edge matching — earns a bonus and a fresh tile.
  • Flagged tiles are requests: grow that landscape to the size on the flag.
  • The run ends when the stack runs out.
