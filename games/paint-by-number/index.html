How to play

Pick a colour, then fill every region carrying that number.

  • Tap a swatch at the bottom to load your brush.
  • Tap — or drag across — any region showing that number.
  • Regions with a different number simply won't take the paint.
  • Pinch, or use ＋ －, to zoom into the fiddly bits. ⤢ resets the view.
  • When a colour runs out the brush moves on by itself.
