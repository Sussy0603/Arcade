How to play

Every pattern here is a real constellation, drawn the way star charts draw it.

  • Drag from one bright star to another to draw a line between them.
  • If the line belongs to the figure it stays lit. If not, it fades away — no penalty.
  • The dim stars scattered around are background sky. Ignore them.
  • Guide lines shows the whole figure faintly. Hint flashes one missing line.
  • Complete a figure and it joins your sky map.
