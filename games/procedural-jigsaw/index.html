How to play

A jigsaw where the picture is drawn by an algorithm the moment you press start.

  • Drag a piece onto the frame. Let go near its home and it clicks into place.
  • Placed pieces lock — you can't knock them loose.
  • Guide shows a faint ghost of the finished picture behind the frame. Turn it off for a harder time.
  • Edges first dims everything except border pieces, the classic way in.
  • Re-scatter tidies the loose pieces if they end up in a heap.
