How to play

Every flower has a partner. Put them together and something better grows.

  • Swipe in any direction and every flower slides as far as it can.
  • Two of the same flower touching will merge into the next species.
  • A new seed appears after every move.
  • Reach the Sunflower to win — then keep going if you like.
  • The garden is over when nothing can slide or merge.
