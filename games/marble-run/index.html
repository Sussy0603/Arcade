How to play

Build a path out of ramps, then release the marbles and watch what happens.

  • Pick a piece from the rack, then tap a square to drop it in.
  • Tap a piece you placed to pick it back up — pieces are never wasted.
  • Press Release marbles to start the flow. Press again to stop it.
  • Fill each jar to the number on it. Marbles that miss simply roll away.
  • You can rebuild while the marbles are running. Nothing is lost.
